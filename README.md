# NextStep — First-Year Student Budgeting Prototype

A product case study: a concept for a Canadian bank feature that helps
new students manage money on their own for the first time, during
their first 30 days away from home/preparing to budget on their own.

## The problem

92% of Canadian post-secondary students say they feel stressed about
money, and only 11% feel financially stable. Several Canadian banks
already have money apps for kids and teens, but those apps are built
around a parent supervising, and that oversight disappears the moment
a teen leaves for university. Nobody's built something for that
specific gap yet.

## What I built

A clickable prototype covering four core features:
- A guided starter budget (3 quick questions, auto-generates a plan)
- A low-balance alert
- A plain-language weekly spend summary
- A savings goal tracker

## Testing it with real people

Tested with 100+ first year students.
Average likelihood to use: **4.2 / 5**. Feedback from the lowest
scorer led to a real product change: a "protected buffer" feature
so the low-balance alert accounts for things like emergency funds
instead of treating the whole balance as spendable.
